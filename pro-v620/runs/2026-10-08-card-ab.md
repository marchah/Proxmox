# Radeon Pro V620: the two cards compared on the ROMED8-2T

Plan committed 2026-10-08 · Run 2026-10-09 · Status: done

## Question

Do the two V620s perform the same on this board? The owner reports `0000:83:00.0` a
little faster than `0000:03:00.0`. Every slot here is CPU-direct Gen4 x16, so a
difference is the card's own. This runs after the telemetry reruns and before the
two-card load-mode and MTP runs, which use its Vulkan-to-card mapping.

- **All on the card:** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B prefill and decode at depths 0, 8192 and 32768,
  and a 4096-token prefill, with each card's clocks, power and junction temperature
  under load.
- **CT 123's production placement:** Qwen3.8-Flash-Next at `-ncmoe 34` on each card in
  the same container. The placement decoded 14.1–15.0 t/s in CT 120 on `03:00.0`
  ([configurations record](../qwen38-flash-next/runs/2026-10-08-configurations.md)) and
  12.7–13.7 in CT 123 on `83:00.0`
  ([`--moe-cache-mib` record](../qwen38-flash-next/runs/2026-10-08-moe-cache.md)); this
  shows how much of that gap is the card.

## What answers it

- Every benchmark and cell completes. The Flash-Next cells show no GTT spill and pass
  the output gate (`SUMMARY.md` flags degenerate output and passes that disagree).
- **Per test:** the median t/s of each card and the difference. A difference counts when
  it is larger than both cards' round-to-round spread (llama-bench) or pass-to-pass
  spread (Flash-Next).
- **Per card:** median busy %, sclk, mclk and power, and the highest junction
  temperature, while the card runs llama-bench; for the Flash-Next cells, the same per
  phase with the CPU cores in use.
- **The CT 120 / CT 123 gap:** if the Flash-Next cells differ by less than their spread,
  the card does not explain it.
- A Flash-Next cell with any DIMM sample at 66 °C is invalid.

## Configurations

| Standard configuration | Cells |
| --- | --- |
| One GPU, on each card | Qwen3.6-35B-A3B under `llama-bench` on `Vulkan0` and `Vulkan1`; Flash-Next `vk0-34` and `vk1-34` (CT 123's production placement) |

The Flash-Next cells are in [`2026-10-08-card-ab.cells`](2026-10-08-card-ab.cells).

- **Two GPUs, CPU only and optimized:** left out. Two GPUs use both cards and CPU only
  uses neither, so neither isolates a card. The optimized `-ncmoe 48` puts most of the
  work on the CPU and would dilute a card difference.

## Controls

- One container (CT 120 holding both cards), one build, the same model files and flags,
  one time window: only the card changes.
- llama-bench rounds alternate which card runs first; the Flash-Next cells run
  round-robin.
- Both cards at 0 mV and 250 W with their blowers under `gpu-blower-control`;
  `environment.json` records both cards' identity, offset, power cap and link.

## Method

Harness: `card-ab.sh` and `qwen38-flash-next/placement-sweep.sh` at the branch head when
the run starts (its commit is in each run's `manifest.json`), staged with
[`push-harness.sh`](../push-harness.sh).

```bash
# Host, after the telemetry reruns; CT 123 stopped
pct start 120
cd /root/harness/<sha12>/pro-v620/qwen38-flash-next
VMID=120 ./install.sh && ./ct120-cutover.sh to-qwen38fn
# Shell 1
VMID=120 ./thermal-guard.sh
# Shell 2
VMID=120 CELLS=../runs/2026-10-08-card-ab.cells DEPTHS="0,8000" ./placement-sweep.sh
cd .. && VMID=120 ./card-ab.sh
```

- **llama-bench** (`card-ab.sh`): `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-UD-Q5_K_XL.gguf` with `-ngl 99 -fa 1
  -b 2048 -ub 1024`, three repetitions per test, three rounds. Tests `-p 512 -n 128 -d
  0,8192,32768` and `-p 4096 -n 0`. The production `-b 4096` does not fit llama-bench
  on this card. The script stops the container's servers first and starts them again
  after.
- **Telemetry:** each card's sclk, mclk, power, junction temperature and VRAM every
  second. The card whose VRAM rose is the card a benchmark ran on, whatever its Vulkan
  index.
- **Flash-Next** (`placement-sweep.sh`): `REPS=3`, `N_PREDICT=256`, prompt classes code,
  list and prose at d0 and d8000 (~5.8k tokens); ctx 65536, parallel 1, 16 threads, q8_0
  KV, batch 4096/1024, projector on the CPU. Each cell's JSON records the card it loaded
  onto.
- Expected duration: about 30 minutes for the Flash-Next cells and 45–60 for
  llama-bench, plus the cutovers.
- Raw data: `/root/qwen38-flash-next/sweep-<timestamp>/` and
  `/root/card-ab/run-<timestamp>/`.

## Safety

- Every other guest stays shut down. CT 123 must stay stopped while CT 120 holds GPU 2;
  the cutover stops it and clears its `onboot`. CT 120 keeps both cards for the two-card
  load-mode and MTP runs; `./ct120-cutover.sh to-qwen36` after the last of them gives
  GPU 2 back. Starting any guest again waits for the owner's go-ahead.
- `thermal-guard.sh` runs with `VMID=120`, watches both cards and stops `llama-bench` or
  `llama-server` on a trip. Neither script starts another benchmark after a trip, and
  both leave the servers stopped.
- llama-bench holds a card at its 250 W cap for minutes at a time.
- The sweep starts no Flash-Next cell while the hottest DIMM is above 58 °C.
- Keep clear of the Sunday 01:00 backup window.

## Results

Both runs used harness `973da4f`. Every llama-bench round and Flash-Next pass completed,
each on the card it was meant for: in CT 120, `Vulkan0` is `0000:03:00.0` and `Vulkan1`
is `0000:83:00.0`. Under llama-bench that is the card whose VRAM rose, which was 0 MiB on
the other card in all twelve runs; in the sweep it is each pass's active card. The
thermal guard did not trip.

## Conclusion

- **llama-bench:** on this board `0000:83:00.0` is the slower card. On Qwen3.6-35B-A3B it
  ran 3.4–4.8% slower in prefill and 1.1–1.3% slower in decode. It held about 115 MHz
  lower sclk than `0000:03:00.0`, with the same clock ceiling, power cap, power profile
  and voltage offset, and drew less power below the cap.
- **Flash-Next:** at `-ncmoe 34`, which leaves most expert weights on the CPU, no
  difference was larger than the spread. Decode was within 0.2 t/s; prefill was 1.1–1.5%
  lower on `83:00.0`.
- **The CT 120 / CT 123 gap:** the card does not explain it. On one card at
  `-ncmoe 34`, CT 120 on `03:00.0` decoded 14.1–15.0 t/s
  ([configurations record](../qwen38-flash-next/runs/2026-10-08-configurations.md)) and
  CT 123 on `83:00.0` 12.7–13.7
  ([`--moe-cache-mib` record](../qwen38-flash-next/runs/2026-10-08-moe-cache.md)), while
  the two cards matched within 0.2 t/s in the same container.
- **The owner's report** that `83:00.0` is faster comes from the prior board. That
  comparison identified the cards by slot, not by `unique_id`, and the owner thinks they
  may have changed places in the move. Its faster card held about the sclk that
  `03:00.0` holds here, and its slower card about `83:00.0`'s.
