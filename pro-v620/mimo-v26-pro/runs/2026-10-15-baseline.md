# MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL: baseline speed on two V620s and 512 GB of DDR4

Plan committed 2026-10-15 · Run after the GLM-5.3-Flash #106 campaign · Status: planned

## Question

How fast does MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL run on this box, in the configuration expected to be
fastest? It is 1.02T total and 42B active, with MXFP4 experts.

On 2026-10-15 the owner asked for basic numbers, not a tuning campaign. The numbers serve
as the baseline for a GPU change and to follow decode speed across hardware upgrades. The
model fills nearly all of the host's RAM, so the record also gives the RAM and VRAM the
configuration leaves free.

## What answers it

Stage 3 times two cells: no speculation (`nospec`) and MTP at stage 2's draft length
(`mtp`). For each, the median of three passes of:

- decode at d0 and d8000, for each prompt class (code, list, prose);
- prefill at d8000;
- aggregate decode at two concurrent streams;
- MTP acceptance;
- per phase, each card's power against its 250 W cap, its clocks, and the CPU cores in use;
- the expert layers on the cards, each card's least free VRAM, CT 123's peak memory, the
  host's least MemAvailable, store reads during the probes, and the hottest DIMM;
- where the RAM experts live: page cache, anonymous memory or GTT, from CT 123's
  file/anonymous split and each card's GTT. The plan expects the page cache (`auto`
  maps the file); stage 1 confirms it.

The **best configuration** is the cell with the higher median d8000 decode over the three
classes, provided every reply is coherent and the contract is clean on every load. Its
numbers are the baseline. Both cells are reported in full. The record sets no speed
threshold; whether the model is usable is the owner's call.

## Stages

**Download.** `mimo-download.sh` fetches the seven shards and both MTP heads, about 558 GB,
and checks each file's SHA-256. It runs in CT 123 after the GLM campaign has restored
Flash-Next. A download during GLM's timed cells would share its 32 pinned cores.

**Stage 1, fit.** One pass over [`-fit.cells`](2026-10-15-baseline-fit.cells), with the
prose class at d0 and d8000 and two streams:

| Cell | Expert layers on cards | Experts in RAM | Checks |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `n67-ub4096` | 3 | 473.3 GiB | the start |
| `n67-cpupp` | 3 | 473.3 GiB | prefill on the CPU (`GGML_OP_OFFLOAD_MIN_BATCH=8192`) |
| `n67-ub2048` | 3 | 473.3 GiB | the compute buffer at a smaller micro-batch, in case a swap at 4096 overflows (GLM #106 stage 2) |
| `n68-ub4096` | 2 | 480.5 GiB | one layer less on the cards |
| `mtp-q8-n68` | 2 | 480.5 GiB | MTP Q8_0 at n-max 2 |
| `mtp-q4-n67` | 3 | 473.3 GiB | MTP Q4_0 at n-max 2 |

`-ncmoe 66` would need 49.1 GiB of weights on the cards before KV and compute buffers. A
second fit pass tries it only if a `-ncmoe 67` cell leaves at least 10 GiB free over both
cards.

**Fit rule, recorded before the fit pass.** A cell fits when all of these hold through its
probes:

1. **VRAM:**
   - on each card, nothing VRAM-requested is outside VRAM in any residency sample;
   - no VRAM allocation fails in `vram-release-trace.sh`'s trace (`vram_new_ret` below 0).
     In GLM #106 stage 2, a compute-buffer swap at micro-batch 4096 failed 25 allocations
     on a card with 5,361 MiB free between swaps;
   - each card's least free VRAM is at least 3,072 MiB outside sub-second compute-buffer
     swaps, and at least 1,024 MiB including them.
2. **RAM** (`page-in-summary.py`):
   - the store reads at most 1 GiB during the probes, i.e. the RAM experts stayed in the
     page cache;
   - CT 123 stays at least 2 GiB under its limit;
   - the host's MemAvailable stays at least 4 GiB.
3. **Output:** the contract is clean and no reply is degenerate.

A cell that holds rule 3, has nothing outside VRAM and reads less than 1 GiB, but misses a
margin, is reported to the owner as margin-only.

**Stage 1 decides, by rules fixed now:**

- **No-speculation placement:** the fitting cell without MTP that has the most expert
  layers on the cards; at equal layers, the larger micro-batch.
- **MTP placement:** the fitting MTP cell with the most expert layers on the cards; at
  equal layers, Q8_0.
- **Op-offload threshold.**
  - Offload copies every RAM expert over PCIe for each micro-batch; the CPU path costs time
    per token.
  - If `n67-ub4096` prefills d8000 faster than `n67-cpupp`:
    - the fixed cost F is `n67-ub4096`'s d8000 prompt time divided by its micro-batches;
    - r is `n67-cpupp`'s d8000 prefill rate;
    - the threshold is F × r, rounded down to a power of two between 32 and 4096.
  - Otherwise it is 8192, and no prompt offloads.
  - A short prompt then takes whichever path is faster.
- **No cell fits:** the campaign stops and the owner decides. The options are a smaller
  file (Q2_K, 390 GiB), a raised GTT limit for `dio`, or fewer slots.

**Stage 2, MTP screen.** One pass at the MTP placement and stage 1's threshold, n-max 1, 2
and 3: all three classes at d0, and two streams. If n-max 3 is fastest, n-max 4 runs as well. The best n-max has the
highest median d0 decode over the three classes. Within 2%, the higher two-stream
aggregate decides. The neighbours of the best length are not timed; that is short of
[TEST-RECORDS.md](../../TEST-RECORDS.md), and follows the owner's basic-numbers scope.

**Stage 3, timed.** `nospec` and `mtp` interleaved, three passes: all three classes at d0
and d8000, and two streams. Each cell uses its stage 1 placement and stage 1's threshold.

## Controls

- `nospec` is the correctness control for `mtp`. Batched verification can change the text,
  so an MTP text that differs is read for coherence, not failed.
- Stage 1's `n67-ub4096` is the reference for the other fit cells.

## Method

**Code.** Each stage runs from this branch's head at its start, staged with
`push-harness.sh`. Its cells file is committed before it runs, and the record names the
commit. Stages 2 and 3 depend on stage 1, so only the fit cells exist at plan time.

**Corpus.** The deep context is the suite's pinned corpus, as in GLM #106: this repository
at `f7e762c53388`, `docs.txt` then `code.txt`, combined SHA-256 `ed17f002…2439`. Depths are
exact token counts cut with MiMo's tokenizer. The file is copied to
`/root/mimo/corpus-f7e762c53388.txt`.

```bash
# Host, as root
H=/root/harness/<sha12>/pro-v620
mkdir -p /root/mimo
# Download, after the GLM campaign's restore. Flash-Next keeps serving meanwhile.
cd $H/glm53-flash && VMID=123 ./install-llamacpp-release.sh   # b11583; a no-op if installed
cd $H/mimo-v26-pro && VMID=123 ./install.sh --download
pct exec 123 -- journalctl -u mimo-dl --no-pager -n 20  # until "DONE all files verified"

# Every guest but CT 123 shut down (Safety), then:
cd $H/glm53-flash && TEST_MODEL=mimo TEST_CPUSET=0-47 ./ct123-test-host.sh two-card
cp /root/glm53-flash/corpus-f7e762c53388.txt /root/mimo/
cd $H/qwen38-flash-next
VMID=123 UNIT=llamacpp-mimo ./thermal-guard.sh          # separate shell
for p in 0000:03:00.0 0000:83:00.0; do
  systemd-run --unit="vram-residency-${p:5:2}" --collect /usr/bin/python3 \
    "$PWD/vram-residency-sampler.py" --pci "$p" --output "/root/mimo/residency-<stage>-${p:5:2}.jsonl"
done
systemd-run --unit=vram-release-trace --collect "$PWD/vram-release-trace.sh" \
  /root/mimo/trace-<stage>.txt
systemd-run --unit=mimo-pagein --collect "$H/mimo-v26-pro/page-in-sampler.sh" \
  /root/mimo/pagein-<stage>.tsv
VMID=123 UNIT=llamacpp-mimo N_BLOCKS=70 CONTRACT_EFFORTS=none,high PROBE_CHAT=true \
  CTX=65536 PARALLEL=2 CONTEXT_FILE=/root/mimo/corpus-f7e762c53388.txt \
  REPS=<n> DEPTHS=<depths> PROBE_CLASSES=<classes> \
  CELLS=../mimo-v26-pro/runs/2026-10-15-baseline-<stage>.cells \
  OUT_DIR=/root/mimo/sweep-<stage>-$(date -u +%Y%m%dT%H%M%SZ) ./placement-sweep.sh
systemctl stop mimo-pagein vram-release-trace vram-residency-03 vram-residency-83
"$H/mimo-v26-pro/page-in-summary.py" /root/mimo/sweep-<stage>-<ts> /root/mimo/pagein-<stage>.tsv

# After stage 3
cd $H/glm53-flash && ./ct123-test-host.sh restore
```

The durations below are estimates, assuming 2–3 t/s decode and 10–100 t/s d8000 prefill.
The first load reads all 554 GB from the store; later loads find it in the page cache.

| Stage | Cells | Passes | Probe | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Download | 9 files, ~558 GB | – | – | hours, at the line's speed |
| 1 fit | 6 (committed) | 1 | `PROBE_CLASSES=prose DEPTHS=0,8000`, `STREAMS=2` | ~1.5–2.5 h |
| 2 MTP screen | n-max 1–3 (+4) | 1 | `DEPTHS=0`, all classes, `STREAMS=2` | ~45 min |
| 3 timed | `nospec`, `mtp` | 3 | `DEPTHS=0,8000`, all classes, `STREAMS=2` | ~3.5–4.5 h |

- **Expected duration:** about 6–8 h of runs after the download. Each stage's results are
  appended when it ends, and the owner can stop after any stage.
- **Raw data** on the host: `/root/mimo/sweep-<stage>-<ts>/`, `residency-<stage>-{03,83}.jsonl`,
  `trace-<stage>.txt` and `pagein-<stage>.tsv`.

## Safety

- **Guests:** CT 123 runs throughout. The probes run from the host, so CT 200 is not
  needed.
  - CT 120 `llamacpp`, CT 121 `hermes`, CT 140 `kb-rag`, CT 200 `bench-runner`, CT 201 and
    VM 300 `docker-host` stay shut down. Hermes has no model for the whole run. Starting any
    of them again waits for the owner's go-ahead.
- **Cards:** `two-card` gives CT 123 both cards and turns CT 120's autostart off.
  `gpu-thermal-watchdog` maps both cards to `llamacpp-mimo`. `restore` gives GPU 1 back and
  restores CT 120's autostart, but leaves CT 120 stopped.
- **RAM:**
  - CT 123's limit is 488 GiB, which leaves the host 15.5 GiB.
  - A server that outgrows the limit is killed inside the container; the host keeps its
    margin.
  - `page-in-sampler.sh` runs through every stage. Rule 2 marks a cell whose experts
    left the page cache.
- **Thermal:**
  - `thermal-guard.sh` runs beside every sweep.
  - The sweep starts no cell above 58 °C on the hottest DIMM. A cell with any DIMM sample at
    66 °C is invalid: the BMC caps memory bandwidth at that temperature, and this test is
    memory-bound.
- **Tracing:** `vram-release-trace.sh` traces both cards and removes its probes on stop. If
  it dies uncleanly, the
  [GLM findings record](../../glm53-flash/runs/2026-10-13-findings.md#safety) has the
  manual cleanup.
- **Backups:** the weekly vzdump runs Sundays at 01:00 ET and covers every guest. No stage
  runs across it.
- **Job timeout:** amdgpu's compute job timeout is 10 s; the deepest request is about 8k
  tokens.
- **Disk:** the store holds 1.75 T. Run `df -h /mnt/models` before the download; the files
  take about 558 GB.
