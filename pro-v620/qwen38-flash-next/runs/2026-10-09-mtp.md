# Qwen3.8-Flash-Next: MTP speculative decoding on one and two cards

Plan committed 2026-10-09 · Run 2026-10-09 · Status: done

## Question

llama.cpp #29761 (in b11475) added a qwen4exp MTP graph, and unsloth publishes separate
MTP heads for this model. On llama.cpp b11505, in CT 120 holding both V620s:

- **Load:** does b11505 load each of the three heads (`--spec-type draft-mtp
  --model-draft`), and how much VRAM does each take on each card?
- **Draft length:** as the head drafts more tokens per step (`--spec-draft-n-max` 1, 2,
  3 and up), where does decode peak, and where does it fall too far to be worth it?
- **Speed:** with the best head and draft length, does MTP raise decode at one and two
  concurrent streams on the fastest one-card configuration (`-ncmoe 34`, CT 123's
  production placement) and the fastest two-card one (`-ncmoe 16`)? A head needs VRAM
  that neither leaves free (about 2 GiB on the one card; 2.9 and 2.0 GiB on two), so a
  head may cost CPU-resident expert layers, about 1.8% of decode and 2.3% of prefill
  each ([configurations record](2026-10-08-configurations.md)). Does MTP gain more than
  that costs?
- **Acceptance:** what share of drafted tokens is accepted, per prompt class?

## What answers it

- **Stage 1, load and screen** (one pass): per head and card count, whether the server
  loads and answers, the VRAM and GTT on each card against the no-head control at the
  same placement, decode and acceptance per prompt cell at one and two streams, and
  whether the output passes the gate. A head that fails to load is reported with its
  error and goes no further.
- **Head choice**, per card count: the head with the highest median single-stream
  decode across the six prompt cells; a smaller head within 2% of it is chosen instead,
  since it needs less room.
- **Stage 2, fit:** the smallest `-ncmoe` at or above the fastest placement (34 on one
  card, 16 on two) at which the chosen head loads with at least 1 GiB of VRAM free on
  each card after a ~5.8k-token request and no GTT growth during the probe.
- **Stage 3, draft-length scan** (one pass): the chosen head at the fitted placement with
  `--spec-draft-n-max` 1 to 8, at one and two streams: median decode per draft length and
  its acceptance. The scan has gone far enough once decode at both stream counts has
  fallen at least 10% below its best length's; if it has not by 8, a second scan adds 10,
  12 and 16.
- **Stage 4, timed:** every cell loads, shows no GTT spill and passes the output gate
  (`SUMMARY.md` flags degenerate output and passes that disagree). Per card count:
  - **Draft lengths:** the scan's best single-stream length and the lengths one below and
    one above it, plus the best two-stream length if it differs.
  - **MTP's own effect:** each against the no-head control at the same placement.
  - **Against the fastest configuration:** the same MTP cells against the no-head control
    at 34 or 16. If the fit is at 34 or 16, the two comparisons are one.
  - Measured by median single-stream decode per prompt class at d0 and d8000, prefill at
    d8000, and per-stream and aggregate decode at two streams. A difference counts when
    it is larger than the pass-to-pass spread of both cells.
  - `SUMMARY.md`'s Draft acceptance table per prompt cell and at two streams, and its
    Utilization table per phase.
  - **Output:** greedy verification keeps the target model's choices, so an MTP cell's
    text is compared with its same-placement control per prompt cell; any difference
    is reported and its text read for sense.
- A cell with any DIMM sample at 66 °C is invalid.

## Configurations

| Standard configuration | Cells |
| --- | --- |
| Two GPUs | `-ncmoe 16`, split by the sweep's rule (`30,18`), with and without the chosen head; screened at `-ncmoe 20` |
| One GPU | `-ncmoe 34` on `0000:83:00.0`, CT 123's card, with and without the chosen head; screened at `-ncmoe 40` |

Stage 1's cells are in [`2026-10-09-mtp-screen.cells`](2026-10-09-mtp-screen.cells).
Stages 2–4's cells (`2026-10-09-mtp-fit.cells`, `2026-10-09-mtp-scan.cells`,
`2026-10-09-mtp.cells`) follow from the stage before and are committed before each runs.

- **CPU only:** left out. It decodes at about half the speed of the two configurations
  a deployment would choose between (7.7–9.1 t/s).
- **Optimized (`-ncmoe 48`):** left out. It hands its VRAM to a second model; spending
  part of it on a head is a separate trade, worth measuring only if MTP wins here.
- **DFlash 2:** left out; none exists for this model. The one repository named for it,
  `tcclaviger/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Dflash2`, holds no weights (checked 2026-10-09).
- **DFlash:** left out. The one drafter, `PixelML/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4-DFlash`, is
  BF16 safetensors with the embedding and output head stripped, so it runs only where the
  engine binds them from the target (vLLM); there is no GGUF. It was trained against the
  NVFP4 target. On its card's own vLLM measurements, at its best block size against the
  native MTP head drafting four tokens, the native head accepted more on code (3.62
  against 2.99 tokens per pass) and chat (2.76 against 2.14).
- **N-gram speculation:** left out. The probe measures fresh generation; n-gram drafts
  pay off on edits of text already in the context, which needs its own workload.
- **Load mode:** `auto` throughout, so MTP is the only change; the
  [two-card load-mode record](2026-10-09-load-modes-two-cards.md) measures `none`.

## Controls

- Each MTP cell has a no-head control at its own placement, and the fastest placement's
  no-head control, from the same binary in the same container and time window.
- Every cell loads at `--parallel 2` so one load gives the single-stream and two-stream
  figures. On one card a second slot used no more VRAM and single-stream decode moved
  beyond the spread in one prompt cell of six
  ([threads record](2026-10-08-threads-concurrency.md)).
- The one-card cells run on `0000:83:00.0` in every stage, the card CT 123 serves from.
- Cells run round-robin, three passes each in stage 4. Stages 1–3 take one pass to
  choose what stage 4 times; their figures are reported but not compared by the spread
  rule.

## Method

Harness: `placement-sweep.sh`, `placement-probe.py`, `concurrency-probe.py` and
`summarize-sweep.py` at the branch head when each stage starts (its commit is in the
run's `manifest.json`), staged with [`push-harness.sh`](../../push-harness.sh). This run
needs the summary's Draft acceptance table and the concurrency probe's acceptance,
added for it. It runs after the [card comparison](../../runs/2026-10-08-card-ab.md)
and the [two-card load-mode run](2026-10-09-load-modes-two-cards.md), with CT 120 still
holding both cards.

```bash
# Host; CT 120 holds both cards after ct120-cutover.sh to-qwen38fn; CT 123 stopped
cd /root/harness/<sha12>/pro-v620/qwen38-flash-next
# Shell 1
VMID=120 ./thermal-guard.sh
# Shell 2: stage 1, then each later stage from the harness that adds its cells
VMID=120 CELLS=runs/2026-10-09-mtp-screen.cells DEPTHS="0,8000" REPS=1 ./placement-sweep.sh
VMID=120 CELLS=runs/2026-10-09-mtp-fit.cells DEPTHS="0,8000" REPS=1 ./placement-sweep.sh
VMID=120 CELLS=runs/2026-10-09-mtp-scan.cells DEPTHS="0,8000" REPS=1 ./placement-sweep.sh
VMID=120 CELLS=runs/2026-10-09-mtp.cells DEPTHS="0,8000" ./placement-sweep.sh
```

- **Heads**, in `/models/hf/qwen3.8-flash-next/`: the self-contained
  `mtp-Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Q8_0.gguf` (4.1 GB) and `-Q4_K_M.gguf` (2.8 GB), re-exported
  upstream on 2026-10-05, and `-shared-Q4_K_M.gguf` (1.9 GB, 2026-09-01), which borrows
  the main model's embedding and output tensors. All three are checksum-verified.
- **Speculation flags:** `--spec-type draft-mtp --model-draft <head> --spec-draft-ngl
  all --spec-draft-n-max N`; n-max 3 in stage 1, as CT 120 runs its own head. The head
  follows the main model's `--device`. `--spec-draft-ngl` defaults to `auto`, which can
  place head layers on the CPU when VRAM is short; `all` makes a head that does not fit
  fail visibly instead. Other speculation settings stay at b11505's defaults, recorded
  in each cell's command line.
- **Fit (stage 2):** start from the head's VRAM in stage 1 and the configurations
  record's 1,501 MiB per CPU-resident layer on one card; load the chosen head at the
  first candidate `-ncmoe`, one pass, and step up one layer until the rule passes.
  Each attempt is a cell in the fit sweep.
- Sweep defaults: `N_PREDICT=256`, prompt classes code, list and prose at d0 and d8000
  (~5.8k tokens); ctx 65536 shared by the two slots, 16 threads, q8_0 KV, projector on
  the CPU. Two cards use batch 1024/256, one card 4096/1024. The concurrency probe runs
  two streams at d0, each a different prompt.
- Expected duration: about 1 hour for stage 1, under 30 minutes for stage 2, about
  2 hours for the scan's 16 cells and 4 hours for stage 4's up to 12, plus the time to
  choose between stages.
- Raw data: `/root/qwen38-flash-next/sweep-<timestamp>/`, one per stage.

## Safety

- Every other guest stays shut down: CT 121 `hermes`, CT 123 `gpu2` (it must stay stopped
  while CT 120 holds its card), CT 140 `kb-rag`, VM 300 `docker-host`, CT 200 and CT 201.
  Afterwards `./ct120-cutover.sh to-qwen36` gives GPU 2 back to CT 123, and starting any
  guest again waits for the owner's go-ahead.
- A head that does not fit fails to load or is flagged for spill; the sweep records the
  cell as failed and moves on.
- The sweep starts no cell while the hottest DIMM is above 58 °C.
- `thermal-guard.sh` runs with `VMID=120` and watches both cards; on a trip the sweep
  starts no further cell.
- Keep clear of the Sunday 01:00 backup window.

## Deviations

- **Stage 2's GTT rule:** the plan asks for no GTT growth during the probe. With the
  Q4_K_M head, GTT grew during the probe even with tens of GiB of VRAM free: in stage 1,
  166 MiB on `03:00.0` while the model ran on `83:00.0`, and 74 MiB on two cards. The fit
  uses the sweep's spill flag instead, which is growth of more than 256 MiB.
- **Two-stream rounds, stages 3 and 4:** in stage 1, the second stream's 22-token prompt
  returned after one token with empty content in three of the six two-stream runs,
  including the no-head control `2c20`. The [threads record](2026-10-08-threads-concurrency.md)
  saw the same in two of nine. The probe had counted such a run as a two-stream
  measurement. From stage 3, each pass runs three rounds (`CONC_REPS=3`), and the
  two-stream medians come only from rounds in which both streams completed.
- **Stop reason:** the probes read `stopped_eos` and `stopped_limit`, which b11505 no
  longer returns, so every row's `stop` read `other` in stages 1 and 2. From stage 3
  they read b11505's `stop_type`.
- **Response text:** the placement probe kept only a hash of each response in stages 1
  and 2. From stage 3 it also keeps the text, so that the output check can read a
  response that differs from its control.
- **Stage 3 on one card, rescanned at `-ncmoe 37`:** stage 2 fitted the head at n-max 3,
  but its VRAM grew about 225 MiB per draft token on one card. At `-ncmoe 36`, n-max 5
  to 8 spilled to GTT during the probe. The one-card scan was run again at 37 with

## Conclusion

All figures are from b11505 on Vulkan, in CT 120 holding both cards, with two slots.

- **Load:** b11505 loads the self-contained Q8_0 and Q4_K_M heads on one and two cards.
  The shared Q4_K_M head fails without the main model's embedding. Q4_K_M decoded within
  2% of Q8_0 and took 985 MiB less VRAM on one card.
- **Single-stream decode:** with the Q4_K_M head at n-max 2 to 4, decode rose in every
  prompt cell beyond the spread, against both the no-head control at the same placement
  and the fastest no-head configuration. Against the fastest, the median of six rose
  30–40% on one card (14.2 to 18.5–19.9 t/s) and 39–45% on two (15.2 to 21.2–22.1).
- **Draft length:** n-max 3 had the highest median of six on one card (19.9 t/s, with 2 at
  19.4) and tied 4 on two (22.1, with 2 at 21.2). No length was fastest in every prompt.
  In the scan, single-stream decode fell beyond 3 and reached 8–9 t/s at 8.
- **Two streams:** n-max 2 and 3 raised the aggregate by 21–25% over the fastest no-head
  configuration on one card and by 25–26% on two. n-max 4 lowered it by about a third on
  both. In the scan, every length from 4 to 8 gave 10.6–15.9 t/s aggregate.
- **Costs:** the head at the same placement cost 1.0–2.8% of d8000 prefill (up to 5.9% at
  n-max 4). Making room for it took two more CPU-resident layers on one card and four on
  two; against the fastest configuration, prefill fell 6.2–10.5% on one card and
  19.6–20.6% on two. At n-max 3 the head took 3.5 GiB of VRAM on one card, growing about 225 MiB per
  draft token.
- **Output:** every head cell's text differed from its control's in every prompt, and the
  no-head controls differed between placements. All texts read as coherent. On the d8000
  prose prompt every head cell skipped the reasoning block that every no-head cell wrote.
- **Two-stream empty replies:** in 27 of stage 4's 90 two-stream rounds, with and without
  a head, the second stream ended after one end-of-sequence token. The two-stream figures
  count complete rounds only.
