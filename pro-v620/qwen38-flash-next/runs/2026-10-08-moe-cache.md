# Qwen3.8-Flash-Next: `--moe-cache-mib` on one V620

Plan committed 2026-10-08 · Run 2026-10-08 18:27–19:57 UTC · Status: done

## Question

Does llama.cpp's MoE expert cache (`--moe-cache-mib`, #29887) run on RADV, and at equal
VRAM headroom, does spending VRAM on cached experts decode faster than spending it on
whole expert layers? What does each configuration cost in prefill?

## What answers it

- **It runs.** Every cache cell logs the cache's size line and a decode
  (`ubatch <= 8`) hit rate, shows no GTT spill, and passes the output gate: no
  degenerate output, and its three reps agree at temperature 0 (`SUMMARY.md` flags
  both, the latter per prompt cell across the three passes). Outputs are not
  compared with the control's: a cached layer's decode matmuls run on the GPU instead
  of the CPU, so greedy text can legitimately differ.
- **It is faster.** A cache configuration's median decode, at d0 and at d8000, beats
  the control's by more than the spread between the control's own three reps.
- **The cost.** Prefill at d8000 for every configuration, against the control.
- **The optimized configuration** keeps at least 12 GiB of VRAM free after its probe;
  its decode and prefill are reported against the control like the others.
- A cell with any DIMM at 66 °C is reported as capped and left out of the
  comparisons.

## Configurations

| Standard configuration | In this test | Entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One GPU | The control: CT 123's production placement, no cache | `34` |
| One GPU, cache variants | VRAM spent on cached experts instead of whole expert layers | `34:auto`, `40:auto` |
| Optimized | Every expert in RAM, with a cache that leaves 12 GiB of VRAM for a second model; also the largest cache RADV can hold at `-ncmoe 48` | `48:leave12288` |
| One GPU, every expert in RAM, VRAM-matched cache | Left out: `48:auto` sizes a ~22.5 GB cache, which RADV cannot allocate (see Method) | — |
| Two GPUs | Left out: the cache refuses to run on more than one device | — |
| CPU only | Left out: the cache needs a GPU | — |

## Controls

- `34` without a cache: CT 123's production placement, on the same build and stack.
- Configurations run round-robin, three reps each. An `auto` cache is sized once per
  placement and reused in every rep.

## Method

Harness: `placement-sweep.sh` at `c5a42e4`, staged with
[`push-harness.sh`](../../push-harness.sh) at `/root/harness/c5a42e409b11/`. The sweep's method rules are in its header
and in [Benchmark tools](../README.md#benchmark-tools).

```bash
# Mac, repo root
./pro-v620/push-harness.sh                  # prints /root/harness/<sha12>/pro-v620
# Host, shell 1
cd /root/harness/<sha12>/pro-v620/qwen38-flash-next && VMID=123 ./thermal-guard.sh
# Host, shell 2
cd /root/harness/<sha12>/pro-v620/qwen38-flash-next
VMID=123 CONFIGS="34 34:auto 40:auto 48:leave12288" DEPTHS="0,8000" ./placement-sweep.sh
```

- Sweep defaults: `REPS=3`, `N_PREDICT=256`, prompt classes code, list and prose,
  `CACHE_MARGIN_MIB=1024`; every cell runs at `-lv 4`.
- Server settings from `qwen38fn-gpu2.env`: ctx 65536, parallel 1, 16 threads, q8_0
  KV, batch 4096, ubatch 1024, projector on the CPU, `--device Vulkan0`.
- `auto` sizing: start the placement without a cache, run one code prompt at d8000
  through the probe, then set the cache to the lower of the free VRAM right after load
  and after that prompt, minus 1024 MiB. `leave12288` uses the same measurement and
  leaves 12,288 MiB instead. Each cell reads VRAM and GTT again after its probe; GTT
  growth over 256 MiB flags a spill.
- RADV limits one allocation to 4 GiB (`maxMemoryAllocationSize`), and the cache keeps
  each expert tensor type in one buffer. At `-ncmoe 48` a 13,000 MiB cache needs a
  4.46 GiB buffer and fails, and 11,302 MiB loads, so `48:leave12288` (11,297 MiB) is
  within about 3% of the largest cache this card can hold there. With llama-server's
  default fit check, an unallocatable cache aborts on a scheduler assertion
  (`ggml-backend.cpp:1084`) instead of a clean error.
- Expected duration: about 1.5–2 hours, from the first round's cell times.
- Raw data: `/root/qwen38-flash-next/sweep-<timestamp>/`, holding `SUMMARY.md`,
  `environment.json`, `manifest.json`, and per-cell JSON, cache and DIMM logs.

## Safety

- Every other guest is shut down for the run: CT 120 `llamacpp`, CT 121 `hermes`,
  CT 140 `kb-rag` and VM 300 `docker-host` (CT 200 and CT 201 are already stopped).
  They stay down when the run ends until the owner says to start them.
- `thermal-guard.sh` runs with `VMID=123`: it watches only card `0000:83:00.0`, acts
  only inside CT 123, and on a trip writes `/root/qwen38-flash-next/THERMAL_TRIP`.
  The sweep then starts no further cell and leaves the server stopped.
- DIMM airflow stays as in the 2026-10-08 soak. The sweep records the hottest DIMM
  per cell.
- At `-ncmoe 48` about 104 GB (97 GiB) of weights stay in host RAM, against CT 123's
  120 GiB memory limit.
- On exit the sweep restores CT 123's env file and restarts its server.
- Keep clear of the Sunday 01:00 backup window.

## Results

Raw data: `/root/qwen38-flash-next/sweep-20261008T182737Z/`. Twelve cells, three
round-robin passes of four configurations, 1 h 29 min.

**Decode**, median of three passes, t/s:

| Config | d0 code | d0 list | d0 prose | d8000 code | d8000 list | d8000 prose |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `34`, no cache (control) | 13.4 | 12.7 | 13.6 | 13.5 | 13.6 | 13.7 |
| `34:auto`, 1,009 MiB | 4.3 | 4.2 | 4.4 | 4.5 | 4.2 | 4.3 |
| `40:auto`, 10,052 MiB | 7.2 | 9.3 | 7.2 | 7.5 | 9.0 | 6.9 |
| `48:leave12288`, 11,297 MiB | 6.0 | 9.4 | 6.2 | 6.4 | 7.5 | 5.9 |

The control's passes ranged 12.0–14.7 t/s across its cells, a spread of up to 15%
within a cell. The fastest pass of any cache configuration was 9.5 t/s.

**Prefill at d8000**, median of the three prompt classes' medians, t/s: control 122.9,
`34:auto` 112.9 (−8%), `40:auto` 104.5 (−15%), `48:leave12288` 96.8 (−21%).

**Memory and cache:**

| Config | Host experts cached | Free VRAM at load / after probe | GTT before → after probe | Decode hit rate | Uploaded per cell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `34` | — | 2,077 / 2,155 MiB | 226 → 263 MiB | — | — |
| `34:auto` | 1.9% of 51,950 MiB; one layer uncached | 1,067 / 1,337 MiB | 229 → 478 MiB | 34.9% | 963 GiB |
| `40:auto` | 16.5% of 60,950 MiB | 1,027 / 1,097 MiB | 230 → 257 MiB | 78.8% | 384 GiB |
| `48:leave12288` | 15.4% of 73,450 MiB | 12,288 / 12,362 MiB | 230 → 255 MiB | 78.8% | 463 GiB |

"Uploaded" is the decode-path (`ubatch <= 8`) upload total for one cell, which decodes
1,536 tokens: about 640, 256 and 309 MiB per token. Hit rates and uploads were
identical in all three passes. `34:auto`'s GTT grew 249 MiB while its free VRAM rose
270 MiB, so it reads as the cache's upload staging, not a spill; no cell was flagged.

**Output gate:** no degenerate output; every configuration produced identical text
in all three passes of every cell. The cache configurations' text differs from the
control's in 17 of 18 cells. All five `reasoning_effort` values were handled, with no
`<think>` in content.

**Thermals:** hottest DIMM 52–53 °C in every cell (cap 66 °C); no thermal-guard trip.

## Deviations

- The guard and the sweep ran as systemd transient units (`qwen38-thermal-guard`,
  `qwen38-moe-cache-sweep`) instead of two interactive shells, with the plan's scripts
  and arguments, so an SSH drop could not end them.
- Four starts preceded the counted run; none of their data is used.
  - 17:22 UTC, harness `877b2dc`: stopped after 8 minutes to shut down the other
    guests. Its `34:auto` cell had been sized from one synthetic request, which left
    2,229 MiB free, and loaded at 877 MiB free.
  - 17:38 UTC, `1a258e0`: stopped after 13 minutes to add the optimized
    configuration. Sizing from a d8000 code prompt alone had loaded `34:auto` at
    948 MiB free.
  - 17:54 UTC, `66b0585`: died at 18:20 loading `48:auto` (22,566 MiB). RADV
    cannot allocate it (see Method), and `48:auto` was dropped.
  - The plan's sizing method and configuration list were updated before the counted
    run; the Method and Configurations sections describe what ran.
- The run's `SUMMARY.md` flags `34:auto` "🔴 cache not active". The sweep's parser read
  `MoE cache budget is too small for 1 layers` as a disabled cache; the cache was
  allocated (1,009 MiB, size line logged). The parser is fixed for later runs.
- `manifest.json` reports `dimms_64gb: 16`: `dmidecode` prints a "Volatile Size" line
  for each DIMM. `environment.json` lists the eight.
- Harness `c5a42e4` is on the branch of PR #97, not on `main`.

## Conclusion

- **It runs:** yes, on RADV with Mesa 26.2.4. Every cache cell allocated its cache,
  logged a decode hit rate, showed no spill and passed the output gate. RADV's 4 GiB
  per-allocation limit caps the cache at about 11.3–11.7 GB at `-ncmoe 48`, well
  under the free VRAM, and an unallocatable cache aborts llama-server's default fit
  check.
- **It is faster:** no. Every cache configuration decoded slower than the control in
  every cell, by more than the control's own spread: medians 4.3, 7.3 and 6.3 t/s
  against 13.6 (−68%, −46%, −54%).
- **The cost:** prefill at d8000 fell 8%, 15% and 21%.
- **Optimized:** `48:leave12288` kept 12.1 GiB free after its probe, against the
  control's 2.1 GiB, at 6.3 t/s decode and 96.8 t/s prefill.
- No cell was DIMM-capped.
